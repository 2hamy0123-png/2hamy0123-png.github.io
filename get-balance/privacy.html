Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short: Balance has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your food, activity and weight logs stay on your phone. The optional team feature stores only encrypted data on a server, and the key to read it never leaves your team's phones.

What stays on your phone

Balance stores these on your device only. The developer never receives them:

  • Your profile: sex, age, height, weight, goal and settings.
  • Your food log, activity log (including routes recorded with GPS) and weight entries.
  • Your reminder times and, if you use a team, your team invite and name.

Uninstalling the app removes this data. You can save a backup file yourself from the Profile screen and restore it later. That file is only as private as the place you keep it.

Camera, location and notifications

  • Camera: used only to read barcodes. Pictures are processed on your phone and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Location: used only while you record an activity, to measure distance and draw your route. While recording, Android shows a notification. Routes are saved on your phone only.
  • Notifications: optional reminders that are scheduled on your phone. No data is sent.

Food lookups

When you scan a barcode or search for a packaged product, the barcode number or the words you typed are sent to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database, to get nutrition facts. Like any website request, this reveals your IP address to them. Nothing else about you is sent. Searching the built-in common foods happens entirely on your phone.

Teams (optional)

If you create or join a team, Balance uses Google Firebase:

  • Anonymous sign-in. The app signs in without an email or password and receives a random anonymous ID.
  • Encrypted content. Your display name, your daily card (whether you logged, whether your day is in range, and your active minutes), your break dates and your messages are encrypted on your phone before they are uploaded. The key exists only in your team's invite code and on members' phones. The developer and the server cannot read the content.
  • What the server can see. The random team ID, the anonymous IDs of members, dates and timestamps, and the size of the encrypted items.
  • Never shared with a team: your calories, food, weight, routes and profile.

Anyone who holds an invite code can join the team and read what it shares, so send invites privately. Leaving a team removes you from its member list. Messages you posted can be deleted in the app before you leave; encrypted daily cards you posted may remain on the server. To ask for them to be deleted, open an issue (see Contact).

Google processes this data under its own terms: Firebase privacy information.

What Balance does not do

  • It does not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • It does not include analytics, crash reporters or other tracking tools.
  • It does not sell or share your data.

Age

Balance sets calorie targets for adults only, and will not create a target for anyone under 18.

Not medical advice

Balance provides general information and estimates. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a doctor or dietitian.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/2hamy0123-png/2hamy0123-png.github.io/issues. Please do not post private health information there.
