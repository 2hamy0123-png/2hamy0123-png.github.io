## Balance, early access

A free Android app for food and activity, with private teams of 3 to 5 friends. No account, no ads.

**Install:** download `Balance.apk` below on your Android phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser. Needs Android 7.0 or newer.

**Updating:** install the new file over the old one. Your data is kept.

**Privacy:** your logs stay on your phone. Read the [privacy policy](https://2hamy0123-png.github.io/get-balance/privacy.html).

**Feedback:** [open an issue](https://github.com/2hamy0123-png/2hamy0123-png.github.io/issues).
